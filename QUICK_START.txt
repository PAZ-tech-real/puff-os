================================================================================
                            QUICK START (3 STEPS)
================================================================================

STEP 1: Build the kernel
   $ cd /workspaces/os
   $ ./dev build
   
   Output: build/kernel.img (50-80 KB)

STEP 2: Create bootable image
   $ ./dev image
   
   Output: build/rpi5-os.img (1 GB, ready to flash)

STEP 3: Flash to SD card
   $ sudo dd if=build/rpi5-os.img of=/dev/sdb bs=4M status=progress
   $ sync
   $ sudo eject /dev/sdb
   
   Insert into Pi5, power on, and watch it boot! 🚀
